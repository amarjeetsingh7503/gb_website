GB ENTERPRISES WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html
- styles.css
- script.js
- assets/ (optimized brochure page visuals)

How to use:
1. Keep the folder structure unchanged.
2. Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.
3. Upload the whole folder to your hosting/server.

Notes:
- Content is based on the supplied GB Enterprises brochure.
- The contact form has not been added because no form-processing/email backend was provided.
- Phone, email and WhatsApp buttons are active.
